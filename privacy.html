Privacy policy

We believe privacy information should be understandable. This policy explains what information may be collected, why it is used and the choices available to you.

Effective date: 27 July 2026   •   Last updated: 27 July 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Fresh Style Culture website and communications that link to it. “We,” “our” and “us” refer to the operator of Fresh Style Culture. This template should be updated with the business’s legal name, registered address and actual service providers before commercial launch.

1. Information you provide

We may collect information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, phone number, delivery and billing information, messages, support enquiries, newsletter preferences, product reviews and information included in an order. If payments are enabled, payment card data should be handled by the selected payment processor; we should receive transaction references and limited payment status information rather than full card details.

Please do not submit passwords, full payment-card details, government identification or sensitive personal information through general contact forms.

2. Information collected automatically

When you use the website, basic technical information may be logged, including IP address, browser and device type, operating system, referring page, pages viewed, approximate region, timestamps and error records. Some data is required for security, network delivery and website operation. Optional analytics data is collected only according to the consent choice presented where required.

3. Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small files stored by your browser. Essential technologies may support security, consent choices, navigation and shopping functions. Analytics cookies help us understand aggregated website use. Advertising cookies, if introduced, would require an updated notice and appropriate choice. You can manage cookies through the consent banner and browser settings; blocking essential storage may affect some functions.

4. Google Analytics

The website includes Google Analytics 4 with measurement ID G-0LY0HY7L01. Consent Mode is configured to keep analytics storage denied by default until a visitor accepts optional analytics cookies. Google may process technical and usage data according to its own terms and privacy information. The production operator should verify that regional consent behaviour and Google settings match actual legal and business requirements.

5. How information may be used

  • To provide, maintain and secure the website;
  • to process and communicate about orders where store functions are enabled;
  • to answer questions and provide customer support;
  • to send requested newsletters and allow unsubscribe;
  • to remember privacy choices and preferences;
  • to analyse performance and improve content when permitted;
  • to prevent fraud, misuse and security incidents;
  • to comply with legal obligations and enforce applicable terms.

6. Legal grounds and consent

Depending on location and context, processing may rely on consent, performance of a contract, compliance with law or legitimate interests such as securing the service and improving it in a proportionate way. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it for the future. Withdrawal does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

7. Sharing and service providers

Information may be shared with vendors that provide hosting, analytics, email delivery, customer support, payment, fraud prevention, order fulfilment or professional advice. Providers should receive only information reasonably necessary for their role and be subject to appropriate obligations. We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect rights and safety, or as part of a business restructuring with suitable safeguards.

We do not sell personal information for money. If future advertising or data practices meet a legal definition of “sale” or “sharing” in a relevant jurisdiction, this policy and user controls must be updated before those practices begin.

8. International processing

Providers may process information in countries other than your own. Privacy protections may differ. Where required, the operator should use recognised contractual or legal safeguards for international transfers and provide additional information on request.

9. Retention

Information should be kept only as long as reasonably necessary for the stated purpose, including legal, accounting, security and dispute requirements. Order records may need longer retention than general enquiries. Consent records may be retained to demonstrate choices. When information is no longer needed, it should be deleted, anonymised or securely isolated according to operational capabilities.

10. Security

Reasonable administrative, technical and organisational measures should protect personal information. However, no internet transmission or storage method is completely secure. Visitors should use trusted devices, protect account credentials and contact us if they suspect unauthorised activity.

11. Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability or withdrawal of consent, and to complain to a privacy authority. Rights can have exceptions. We may need to verify identity before responding. You can unsubscribe from marketing emails using the included link; necessary service messages may still be sent.

12. Children’s privacy

The website is intended for a general adult audience and is not knowingly directed to children below the age at which independent data consent is valid. If a parent or guardian believes a child provided personal information, they should contact us so it can be reviewed and removed where appropriate.

13. External links

The website may link to third-party services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own notices. A link does not mean we control or endorse those practices, so review external policies before providing information.

14. Policy changes

We may update this policy to reflect service, legal or operational changes. The revised version will show a new update date. Material changes may receive an additional notice where appropriate. Continued use after an update is subject to the revised policy, except where fresh consent is legally required.
