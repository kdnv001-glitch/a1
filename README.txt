FRESH STYLE CULTURE — WEBSITE PACKAGE

Main homepage: index.php
Other pages: HTML

UPLOAD
1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Upload all contents of the freshstyleculture folder to public_html.
3. Confirm your hosting supports PHP 7.4 or newer.
4. Open your domain; index.php will load automatically.

IMPORTANT BEFORE COMMERCIAL LAUNCH
- Replace placeholder email addresses.
- Connect the contact and newsletter forms to your preferred backend/service.
- Replace illustrative products, prices and remote image URLs with your own licensed content.
- Review Privacy Policy and Terms with a qualified local professional.
- Configure your real checkout, shipping, return and payment terms.
